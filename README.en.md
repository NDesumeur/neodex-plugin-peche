# Pêche (Fishing)

A fishing game: cast your line, wait for the bite, strike at the right moment and tire the fish out to reel it in. 153 species across 7 places, each with its own hours, weather and favourite baits, a shop for rods and baits, a collection, daily challenges and an angler level.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## Fishing, step by step

1. **Cast**: hold the click or **Space** to set the power, release to cast. A longer cast gives a better chance of a rare fish, but you will have to reel it in from further away.
2. **Wait**: the float sometimes twitches for nothing. Wait for the **real bite**.
3. **Strike**: click fast on the bite. A **perfect strike** (within a quarter of a second) already tires the fish and brings it closer.
4. **Fight**:
   - **hold** the click or Space to reel in;
   - **let go** when the fish pulls hard, otherwise the tension rises and the line breaks;
   - when it runs left or right, **counter** with ← → (or the mouse) the other way: you reel faster, with less tension, and it tires;
   - big sporty fish **jump**: let go during the jump to tire it all at once;
   - a worn-out fish comes in quickly. If the line stays slack too long, or it swims too far, it escapes.

You can also reel in an old boot or a **treasure chest** (coins and 5 baits).

## Places

| Place | Price |
|---|---|
| Willow pond | free |
| Clear river | 600 coins |
| Mountain lake | 1,800 coins |
| Rocky coast | 3,500 coins |
| Tropical lagoon | 8,000 coins |
| Ice floe | 14,000 coins |
| Abyss | 30,000 coins |

The further the place, the more its fish are worth. Time passes in the game (one real second is one minute): dawn, day, dusk and night, with weather that changes by place (clear sky, cloudy, rain, storm, fog, snow).

## Fish

Each species has a rarity (common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary), a minimum and maximum size, places, hours (by day, at dawn and dusk, at night, or at any time), sometimes a weather (in the rain, in fine weather, when it snows) and favourite baits, which make it bite much more often.

A fish sells according to its rarity, its size compared with its species and the place. A **streak** of catches without a miss raises the payout, up to +50 %.

## The shop

- **Rods**: bamboo (starting rod), fibre (400), carbon (1,800), competition (6,000) and stellar (18,000). A better rod holds more tension, reels faster and attracts rare fish more.
- **Baits**: bread (free and unlimited), earthworms, maggots, shrimps, lure, squid and glowing bait, sold in packs of 10 to 25 casts. Some also make fish bite faster.
- **Places**: to unlock with your coins.

## Collection, challenges and level

- **Collection**: each species discovered, with its card (places, hours, weather, baits) and your biggest catch; the others stay hidden.
- **Challenges**: three challenges a day, rewarded with coins (catch a number of fish, a rare fish, land perfect strikes, a catch close to maximum size, a new species, a streak, fish at night, a chest).
- **Angler level**: each catch gives experience (more for a rare fish, a new species or a perfect strike). Each level reels a little faster and attracts rare fish a little more.

## Data and sync

The whole game (coins, gear, places, collection, challenges, level and game time) is stored by NeoDex, backed up and synced between your PCs when sync is on. On first launch, the plugin picks up what you already had in NeoDex.
